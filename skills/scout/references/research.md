# Research, the short form

## Where the truth lives
| Question | Go first to |
|---|---|
| What a library or API does, its limits, its version | its own docs, source, changelog, issue tracker (context7 for docs) |
| What a platform costs, its quotas, its terms | the vendor's own pricing, limits, and terms pages, with the date |
| Whether something is feasible | a minimal spike, then the maker's docs |
| What changed in an upgrade | the release notes and migration guide of each version crossed |
| How existing code behaves | running it and its tests, then reading it |
| What practitioners report | several independent reports, never one post |

## Grades (every decision-driving claim gets one, plus the access date)
- **primary**: the maker's own docs, code, data, or filing.
- **triangulated**: two independent sources agree (two copies of one press release are one source).
- **single-source**: one secondary source.
- **inferred**: reasoned from evidence, not found stated.
- **contested**: sources disagree; both are listed.

## Laws
- Triangulate what a decision rests on; a single blog is weak evidence.
- Search deliberately against your leading answer: look for why it fails, who abandoned it, what broke.
- Fresh beats remembered: a fact about prices, limits, versions, or availability is fetched now, with its date.
- Compute, do not estimate, when the numbers are available.
- Report what you did not find as clearly as what you found.
